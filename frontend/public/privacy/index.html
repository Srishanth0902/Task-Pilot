Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Task Pilot is a calendar assistant operated by the Task Pilot project team. For privacy questions or a request to delete your stored account data, contact netflixchill3007@gmail.com.

Information we use

Google sign-in provides your account identifier, name, email address and profile picture. With your permission, Task Pilot accesses Google Calendar to read, search, create, move, update and delete events in response to your requests. Relevant event information can include titles, dates, descriptions, locations and attendee details.

We store your Google OAuth tokens, account profile, conversation history and agent state, scheduling preferences, and any assignments and study-session records you create. We also store session and usage information needed for sign-in, request limits and reliable operations.

AI processing and service providers

When you use the assistant, your message, recent conversation context, scheduling preferences and relevant calendar information may be sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider to interpret your request. OAuth tokens and application API keys are not included in the planner context. AI routing excludes providers marked by OpenRouter as allowing data collection; if no compatible provider is available, the request fails rather than relaxing that restriction.

Google handles authentication and Calendar operations. Render hosts the application and Neon hosts its production database. These providers may process technical metadata such as network addresses, request times and usage records under their own policies. We do not promise that all processing takes place in India or that providers retain no technical records.

Read the policies of Google, OpenRouter, Render and Neon. Email reminders are currently disabled.

Use of Google data

Google data is used to provide the calendar and scheduling features you request, maintain your conversations, and keep the service working securely. Task Pilot does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or training generalized AI models. Transfers to AI and infrastructure providers are for providing these user-facing features. Task Pilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, security and retention

Stored profiles, OAuth tokens, conversation content, preferences and assignment content are encrypted at the application layer. Some operational identifiers, timestamps and counters remain readable to the database service. Public connections use HTTPS. These protections do not guarantee that security incidents are impossible.

Saved accounts and conversations remain stored until deleted by the project operator in response to a verified request; there is no automatic account or conversation deletion schedule. Sign-out ends your session but does not delete your saved data or revoke Google access. Session expiry is renewed with active use. Operational logs and provider backups may persist separately; we cannot promise immediate removal of all backup copies.

Your choices

You may stop using Task Pilot, sign out, or revoke its access in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized Calendar access but does not automatically remove stored chats. Email the contact above to request deletion of your stored account, tokens and conversations; we will need to verify that you own the account. Deleting Task Pilot data does not delete events already created in Google Calendar.

Do not include passwords, medical details or other sensitive information you do not want processed by the assistant. We may update this policy when the application changes and will update the effective date on this page.
