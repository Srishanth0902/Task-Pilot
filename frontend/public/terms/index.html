Task Pilot · Privacy policy

Terms of use

Effective 4 October 2026

Task Pilot is an experimental project that helps you manage your Google Calendar using natural-language requests. It is operated by the Task Pilot project team. Contact netflixchill3007@gmail.com for support.

Using your calendar

Connect only accounts you are authorized to use. Your requests can create, change or delete calendar events. Review event details, proposed changes and confirmation requests carefully. Confirmations are required for bulk changes, but not every individual operation requires confirmation. AI can misunderstand a request; check Google Calendar after important changes and before retrying an interrupted request.

Availability and limitations

The current deployment uses free hosting and AI services. Cold starts, usage limits, outages or provider changes may delay or prevent requests. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability, perfect interpretations or successful execution of every request. Do not rely on this project as your only reminder system for emergencies or critical obligations.

Responsible use

Do not abuse request limits, attempt to access other users' data or use Task Pilot for unlawful purposes. Keep your Google account secure and sign out on shared devices. Data handling and third-party processing are explained in the privacy policy.

Stopping use

You can sign out or revoke Google's permission at any time. Contact support to request deletion of stored Task Pilot data. Removing access or deleting application data does not reverse calendar changes already made. Features and these terms may change as the project develops; this page will show the updated effective date.
